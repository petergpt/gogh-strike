## Portrait observations

The [Met’s *Portrait of the Artist*, 1878](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/10409) was opened and its actual image inspected. It shows a rounded cheek and chin, a defined nose, swept-up hair beneath an ornamented hat, and a high light collar. Its seated asymmetry belongs to the painting; it is not evidence of her gait.

The [National Portrait Gallery’s *Self-Portrait*, c. 1880](https://npg.si.edu/object/npg_NPG.76.33) was inspected through the Art Institute of Chicago’s [museum-hosted reproduction](https://artic-web.imgix.net/null28a55ead-0551-43f3-bdb7-cad3f126df0c/NPG-NPG_76_33Cassatt_d1.jpg?auto=format%2Ccompress&crop=faces%2Ccenter&fit=crop&h=2152&q=80&rect=0%2C0%2C2974%2C4000&w=1600). The direct NPG object page rejected access. The opened [Smithsonian teaching guide, pp. 66–67](https://npg.si.edu/sites/default/files/npg_women-curriculum_guide_interactive-fields_final.pdf#page=66) independently identifies the work and discusses its returned gaze. The self-portrait predates the setting by about eight years; the Met work by ten.

Three face cues guide the sculpture:

- **Full cheek planes and a rounded, definite chin:** visible in both self-portraits; `headWidth 1.07`, `headLength 0.99`, `cheekFullness 0.67`, `chinWidth 0.98` retain adult structure without a tiny pointed lower face.
- **A substantial, clearly bridged nose:** especially legible in the c. 1880 work; `noseLength 1.06`, `noseWidth 1.03`, `noseBridge 1.03`, `noseTip 0.98` preserve that presence without a bulbous caricature.
- **Defined eyes and modest lips:** the c. 1880 eye contours and shadows suggest `eyeSize 0.92`, `eyeDepth 0.43`, `lidWeight 0.55`, with `mouthWidth 0.96` and `lipFullness 0.94`. Age treatment `0.49` is a restrained 1888 extrapolation, not a claim that all pictured shadow marks are wrinkles.

## Gameplay silhouette and invention

1. A broad, tilted **plum portrait hat**, with the assigned asymmetric blue-grey ribbon and feather, makes the head silhouette distinct from Morisot’s compact bonnet. The exact hat construction, plum hue and feather are fictional; the references support elaborate headwear, not this exact object.
2. A **curved layered capelet over a high ivory collar** makes one rounded shoulder envelope above a settled waist. Muted velvet, wide draping folds, restrained seams and horn buttons replace a flat colour shell. This costume is art direction; its cloth type is not established by the portraits.
3. **Fuller waist and hips, regular-length legs and pinned hair** give Cassatt a substantial adult silhouette without broad masculine shoulder geometry or exaggerated hourglass proportions. This deliberate contrast to Morisot is not a documented weight or height claim. Cuffed sleeves avoid toy-like shoulder puffs.

The dark plum outer layer, warm ivory collar, blue-grey hat ribbon and dusty rose trim form one palette. Hair is warm brown, with subdued eye colour and skin approximations; neither painting establishes an exact natural colour specification.
